# bleibtgleich’26: Design reference study

## Scope and evidence

The reference is the [Awwwards page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bleibtgleich26).
This study uses its screenshots, metadata, and linked Awwwards motion previews.
The study records the displayed portfolio and the Awwwards page frame separately.
The design principles below are my interpretation of the visible work.
They are not statements from the designers.

I inspected the Main Page, Navigation, Contact, Experiment, and One Project previews.
I also inspected the Grid and Poster thumbnails and the Work video on the reference page.
The previews do not establish the complete behavior of the live site.
They do not establish animation durations, easing curves, mobile behavior, or reduced-motion support.

## Visual style and mood

The portfolio combines Swiss typography, an editorial grid, and experimental digital effects.
The mood is spare, confident, personal, playful, and slightly strange.
Large black words give the design authority.
Small labels and large empty areas give it restraint.
The mask symbol and unusual interactions give it personality.
The result resembles a graphic poster that becomes an interactive gallery.

The white home scene feels controlled and quiet.
The colored project scenes feel more intense.
The scattered experiment scene feels like a collection of objects in space.
The contact scene feels like a mechanical instrument.

## Design principles

1. **Make type the main visual element.** Large, heavy sans-serif words carry the identity and message.
2. **Use strong scale contrast.** Small role labels sit beside much larger names and statements.
3. **Use tight type spacing.** Close letter spacing and short line spacing make the large words feel like solid shapes.
4. **Use line breaks as composition.** The design splits phrases and words to fit narrow columns and create rhythm.
5. **Use an asymmetric grid.** Text blocks occupy different columns and start at different heights.
6. **Keep empty space active.** Large clear areas create tension around the text and images.
7. **Use thin rules for structure.** Fine vertical lines define divisions without heavy boxes.
8. **Keep the base palette simple.** Awwwards lists white, `#FFFFFF`, and black, `#000000`.
9. **Let projects introduce color.** Red project scenes and the olive Work scene contrast with the white home scene.
10. **Repeat the identity in small details.** The mask and the pale corner words connect different scenes.
11. **Use few visible controls.** A small Menu control leaves space for the work.
12. **Turn navigation into a graphic object.** Numbered panels form a stepped composition.
13. **Make interaction part of the portfolio.** The menu, gallery, and contact dial demonstrate visual and technical skill.
14. **Balance geometry with organic shapes.** Straight rules and rectangular images contrast with the irregular mask and fluid effects.
15. **Use depth to separate objects.** Different image sizes, positions, and opacity levels give the experiment scene several visual planes.
16. **Carry the graphic system across media.** The Poster thumbnail repeats the type composition on paper in a natural setting.

These principles derive from the [reference page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bleibtgleich26) and its Awwwards previews.

## Motion and interaction inventory

### Main Page

The preview places project images over the large home typography.
An irregular pale shape appears around the image area.

This gives a simple page a sense of physical response.

### Navigation

The preview shows three numbered gray panels with different heights.
The panels contain Home, Work, and Contact.

The preview does not establish the exact order or duration of each panel movement.

### Work

The reference video shows an olive scene with Work between the year labels.

The preview opens with a red project scene and a large central project name.

### Experiments

The preview shows many images at different sizes and opacity levels.
Their positions change between captured states.
The large central mask image gives way to other images as the scene moves.
This creates the impression of travel through a spatial collection.

The preview does not establish whether the visitor controls this movement through scroll, pointer movement, or another input.

### Contact

The preview uses a rotary telephone dial as the contact interface.
The central mask reverses to white on black.
Small circular controls contain contact and social symbols.
One control appears black while nearby controls remain pale.
Two thin horizontal rules extend outward from the dial.
The contact scene turns a common final step into a distinct visual object.

The captured states establish the dial and control contrast, but not a complete rotation sequence.

Source: [Contact preview](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/contact-bleibtgleich26).

## Motion principles

These are inferred principles, not measured animation settings.

- **Use motion to change composition.** Move between columns, depth, grids, and individual project scenes.
- **Keep a stable identity during scene changes.** Repeat the menu, mask, typography, and corner words.
- **Contrast rigid and fluid behavior.** Place organic effects against straight rules and dense type.
- **Give each section its own motion idea.** Use panels for navigation, depth for work, and a dial for contact.
- **Keep motion connected to content.** Animate the projects and controls that the visitor explores.

Awwwards lists GSAP, Webflow, and Filters and Effects on the [reference page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bleibtgleich26).
These labels do not establish which tool creates each effect.

## The Awwwards page frame

The Awwwards interface has a different visual system from the displayed portfolio.
Its page uses a pale gray background, dark rounded preview cards, and a regular two-column gallery.
Captions sit below the cards and link to element categories.
A fixed lower bar provides access to the page sections and the external site.
Yellow and orange controls emphasize visits and votes.
The page also includes a horizontal promotional marquee.

This frame presents the portfolio as a curated exhibit.
The dark preview cards help the white portfolio screenshots stand out.
The category labels let the visitor study individual design elements.

Source: [Awwwards reference page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bleibtgleich26).

## Reference direction

Use large, tightly spaced black type on white.
Arrange the type in uneven columns with thin rules and large empty areas.
Repeat one unusual identity symbol.
Give projects their own colors and spatial scenes.
Use distinct motion for the menu, project gallery, experiment collection, and contact interface.
Keep the graphic structure clear while the motion adds surprise.
