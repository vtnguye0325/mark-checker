Outlined and left of the axis means the field pushes against distinctiveness. A filled bar to the right would push toward it. Fill and side carry the sign, so the chart reads without color.

Why it lands there

Both halves of the mark are ordinary English words that name a quality of the goods. A granola bar that is crisp and crunchy is described, not branded, by "CRISPCRUNCH". Joining the two words without a space does not create a new meaning, so the compound is read as its parts.

The goods description made this worse rather than better. Against a class 30 snack description the mark's words are directly on the nose, which is the test the office applies.

A mark is merely descriptive if it describes an ingredient, quality, characteristic, function, feature, purpose, or use of the specified goods. TMEP § 1209.01(b) — Merely Descriptive Marks

What to do next

Move up the rail. Replace one half of the compound with a word that has no connection to snack food, and the mark crosses into the arbitrary band without losing the sound you want. Keeping "CRISPCRUNCH" means filing under section 2(f) and proving five years of use first.
