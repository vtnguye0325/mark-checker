Analysis

What the model found

ZEPHYRLINE is a coined term. It carries no dictionary meaning and no recognized association with drinkware, so nothing in the mark describes the goods. The classifier returns 0.94 with high confidence.

What the model leaned on

The mark text carried the verdict almost alone. Blanking it drops the score by 0.41, more than every other field combined.

The goods description pulled back very slightly. "Insulated water bottles" is a plain functional phrase, and marks paired with plain goods descriptions score a little lower on average.

The doctrine

Fanciful marks comprise terms that have been invented for the sole purpose of functioning as a trademark. Such marks are the strongest and are afforded the widest latitude of protection. TMEP § 1209.01(a) — Fanciful, Arbitrary, and Suggestive Marks

What to do next

Distinctiveness is only the first gate. Run a conflict search against registered marks in class 21 before you file, and keep the coined spelling — adding a descriptive word such as "hydro" or "thermo" would move the mark down the spectrum.
